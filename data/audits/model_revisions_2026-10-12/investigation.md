# YOLO cafeteria — diagnosis and reproducible experiment plan (2026-10-12)

## Scope and evidence

This review uses the repo's 11-class schema, its audit reports, four visually inspected LVIS photos, the 55-image locally run inference comparison supplied by the user, and a direct machine audit of all 1,364 proposed LVIS food-overlay boxes. The trained `best.pt`, original generated validation dataset and per-image `progress.jsonl` caches remain on the user's computer, not GitHub, so this environment cannot run new GPU inference or claim a gain in model AP.

## Established baseline and limitations

- Original 100-epoch YOLO11n run: reported mAP50 **0.779**, mAP50–95 **0.573**, on 619 validation images. Only seven of the 11 classes have targets in validation; paper boats have **zero train images**. These are provisional public-source validation metrics, **not NHS performance**.
- Source-level class weakness: validation AP50 plate **0.559**, wrapper **0.435**, bottle **0.642**. Wrapper has just 19 validation objects; that uncertainty is substantial.
- The 55-image inference cohort contains 47 labeled plates, 19 wrappers and 24 bottles (90 targets). At confidence 0.25 and IoU >= 0.50: full 640px **45/90**; full 960px **44/90**; full 640px plus 4 crops and class-aware NMS **53/90**. The corresponding *unmatched predictions* are 31, 22 and 92, respectively. Because labels are incomplete, do **not** identify all unmatched boxes as model false positives. These fixed-confidence detection recalls are **not mAP**.
- Large resolutions are **not** a demonstrated fix. Slicing gains 8 matches but incurs roughly 3x as many unmatched candidates. It is also computationally more expensive; actual MPS latency has not been measured.

## New direct dataset audit

All 242 revised LVIS overlay records were inspected programmatically. They contain 1,364 official LVIS `food` boxes (1,158 in 209 train photos; 206 in 33 validation photos). Box classes and normalized geometry passed structural checks. **856/1,364 (62.8%)** cover under 1% of an image. These tiny boxes may be particularly challenging; geometry validity alone does not prove semantic completeness.

The original processed LVIS plate labels are still *plate-only* in many cases. Examples visually checked: `train__lvis_000000225490.jpg` has pizza slices on plates; `train__lvis_000000478368.jpg` has eight mini pizzas on a tray; `val__lvis_000000557582.jpg` has two people holding pizza with only a thin plate edge visible. The proposed food overlay **does include** 5 pizza boxes for 225490, 8 for 478368 and 2 for 557582. Do not mistakenly claim those food boxes are still absent from the **revised** experiment, or directly compare the old 0.779 AP to scores on changed validation ground truth.

Cross-box screening found 96 pairs with >90% containment of one food box inside another across 23 images, of which **60 ingredient labels in seven images** satisfy a narrower topping-within-composite review heuristic (50 training, 10 validation). Examples include peppers/tomatoes contained by a `bread` box and blueberries contained by a `cake` box. A review queue was committed to `data/audits/model_revisions_2026-10-12/ingredient_review.csv`. **They are candidates, not certified bad labels, and none were deleted**. Decide consistently whether a cafeteria food “instance” is the complete serving or each distinguishable food ingredient before changing anything.

## Engineering changes committed

`scripts/analyze_cached.py` uses the two existing JSONL caches, sweeps 640px, 960px and crop confidence / NMS settings without additional inference, and chooses a candidate on a diagnostic development subset. The matcher now finds a **maximum number of distinct same-class GT assignments** at IoU>=0.5; a previous greedy matcher could undercount overlapping plates. A source-group hashing rule keeps multiple Roboflow exports of one photo on the same side of the diagnostic split. It reports per-class, per-source, and size-stratified recall and produces a confidence-ranked unmatched review queue. The optional four-panel overlays show the **same image** with labels, 640px inference, 960px inference, and 640px crops/NMS. These are *measurement and triage improvements*, not proof the deployed detector is more accurate.

Run from repo root on the Mac (cache files already generated by the earlier resolution run):

```bash
git pull
python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -v
python3 scripts/analyze_cached.py --review-overlays 15
```

Review outputs: `model/runs/crop-diagnostic/cached_analysis.json`, `cached_review_queue.csv`, and `cached_review_overlays/*.jpg`. The diagnostic holdout still comes from the existing, potentially correlated public-source validation set. The selection budget controls unmatched-candidate workload, not real-world precision.

## Highest-value next interventions (in order)

1. **Complete clean evaluation data.** Annotate all 11 configured classes (including difficult visible objects) on a source/capture-group-disjoint NHS set; separate tuning validation and final test. Paper boats need real labeled training images before this class can work.
2. **Human-review the top missed/unmatched cases.** Classify high-confidence unmatched boxes as real unlabeled objects, wrong class, duplicates or real background. Do not auto-add labels from predictions without human checks. Prioritize actual cafeteria tray photos, not only LVIS and roadside litter.
3. **Resolve portion/ingredient label policy** for the 60 queued LVIS cases. Rebuild revised label copies and evaluate *all compared models* against the exact same corrected validation labels.
4. **Evaluate inference on frozen clean labels.** Test no slicing vs conservative class-aware crops, varied per-class thresholds, and fixed latency. Higher resolution alone has already failed this 55-image proxy test.
5. **Controlled training experiment.** Run standard YOLO11n and experimental source-aware loss from identical `yolo11n.pt`, the same reviewed dataset, split, seed, training schedule, optimizer and input resolution. This probes the missing-label hypothesis without confounding the comparison with a bigger model or altered validation labels. The masked loss is *experimental*, requires pinned Ultralytics 8.4.175, and should first receive a short 10-epoch integration run.
6. **Class diversity before repetition.** Acquire real wrappers (particularly small packaged snacks), paper boats, napkins, bowls and condiment packets under varied backgrounds/occlusions. Repeating 44 condiment images does not create independent scenes. Candidate external datasets must be reviewed for license, near duplicates, coverage and NHS similarity before incorporation.

## Relevant research

- Akyon et al. (ICIP 2022), **Slicing Aided Hyper Inference**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.06934 — motivates slice experiments, not a guaranteed gain for our model.
- Yang et al. (2020), **Object Detection as a Positive-Unlabeled Problem**: https://arxiv.org/abs/2002.04672 — explains confusion from missing foreground boxes treated as background.
- Gupta et al. (CVPR 2019), **LVIS**: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Gupta_LVIS_A_Dataset_for_Large_Vocabulary_Instance_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf — federated positive/negative image/category coverage rather than a claim of exhaustiveness for every category.
- Kaul et al. (CVPR 2022), **Label, Verify, Correct**: https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/html/Kaul_Label_Verify_Correct_A_Simple_Few_Shot_Object_Detection_Method_CVPR_2022_paper.html — human verification of pseudo-annotations matters.
- Ultralytics model validation: https://docs.ultralytics.com/modes/val — native AP, PR, mAP size breakdown and confusion matrices.
- Candidate wrapper/bottle dataset to **inspect, not auto-import**: https://universe.roboflow.com/convert-coco-73sdy/trash_collection_self (569 images; verify annotations and license on its dataset page).


## October 12 cached-inference results (55 images, 90 target objects)

User-run `cached_analysis.json` evaluated 25 parameter combinations, partitioning the
same **provisional** validation cohort into 31 development images (61 targets)
and 24 diagnostic holdout images (29 targets). This is not an independently
collected NHS holdout.

At confidence 0.25, ordinary 640px inference matched **45/90** objects with
**31 unmatched predictions**. Full-image 960px matched **44/90** with 22
unmatched predictions. Crops with confidence 0.25 and NMS IoU 0.50 matched
**53/90** with 92 unmatched predictions.

Development-only selection chose crop confidence 0.35, NMS IoU 0.30:
- Development: **32/61**, vs original 640px **28/61**; unmatched predictions
  **35 vs 21**.
- Diagnostic holdout: **17/29**, exactly the baseline's **17/29**; unmatched
  predictions **14 vs 10**.
- All images: **49/90 vs 45/90**; unmatched **49 vs 31**.

The selected crop setting recovered one wrapper but lost one plate on the
diagnostic holdout. NMS IoU 0.30 is aggressive in overlapping-plate scenes:
changing to IoU 0.50 recovered that plate, but raised the holdout unmatched
count from 14 to 24. These trade-offs **do not establish better deployed
precision or NHS generalization**. Notably, **34 of 37 sub-1%-area targets**
landed in the development split, with only three on the diagnostic holdout;
tiny-object performance cannot be judged reliably from that holdout alone.

### Confirmed label-to-prediction confusion

Review of actual images on GitHub and the exported prediction locations shows
at least **three high-confidence bottle predictions** over regions annotated
as wrappers (IoU with the other class 0.922, 0.934, and 0.874; confidence
0.635, 0.676, and 0.621). One source image contains a flattened blue food
packet; another contains discarded orange and green food packets alongside an
actual beverage bottle. This indicates **wrapper/bottle class confusion**
rather than solely insufficient image resolution. Correct category definitions
and difficult cross-class examples should be human reviewed; never rewrite
annotations just because a model predicts another label.

### Threshold-analysis bug repaired

The initial `scripts/analyze_cached.py` filtered full-frame predictions at
confidence 0.10-0.20 but then silently matched only predictions at or above
0.25. As a result, **the initial report's low-confidence recall rows are
invalid**; they misleadingly showed identical recall at 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 and
0.25. Commit `ae61c377` removes the second threshold after explicit
filtering; a regression test was added. Comparisons above at confidence
0.25 or above are not affected. Re-run the script over the existing JSONL
cache before selecting confidence thresholds; no model inference or training
is needed.

## Stop rules

Do not claim a 90%+ accuracy gain from changing confidence thresholds on an incompletely labeled 55-image proxy. Do not edit original labels or test images based on model guesses; do not mix test data into training. Retain each experiment's pretrained checkpoint, data hashes, train/validation lists, label-policy version, epoch/step budget and per-class metrics. For any claimed improvement, publish paired results on exactly the same fully annotated, independent NHS test set, and latency on target hardware.
