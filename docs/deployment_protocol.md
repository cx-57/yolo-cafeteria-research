# Deployment protocol

## Stage 1 — shadow deployment
Observe tray/waste scenes and record predictions without directing real disposal behavior.

Record prediction, disposal recommendation, confidence, latency, ground truth, error type, and environmental condition.

Frame the camera to the scan area and avoid unnecessary identifying imagery.

## Stage 2 — optional live guidance
Only after shadow-deployment performance is acceptable and school approval is in place. Human-behavior experiments are an extension, not required for the core CV paper.
