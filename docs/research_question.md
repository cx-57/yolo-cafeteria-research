# Research question

> **To what extent can a real-time YOLO-based computer vision system provide accurate and reliable disposal guidance under realistic school cafeteria conditions?**

## Operational definitions

- **Accurate:** correct item detection and, more importantly, correct disposal recommendation.
- **Reliable:** performance remains useful under clutter, occlusion, food residue, deformation, and varied lighting.
- **Real-time:** end-to-end camera-to-guidance latency is fast enough for a cafeteria disposal workflow.
- **Realistic school cafeteria conditions:** unseen NHS-style scenes with actual food, serviceware, packaging, leftovers, and object overlap.
